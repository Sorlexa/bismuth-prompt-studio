# Style terms

Everything the **style hint** field understands. Type any of these (several, separated by commas); the field's autocomplete offers the same list. A term that names a style, a medium, a light or a technique is written into the prompt; a *look* (the second list) also steers which artists are matched. Rebuilt with every release by `tools/build/write_style_terms.py`.
